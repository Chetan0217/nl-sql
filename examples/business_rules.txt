# Business rules for the nlsql_demo retail database.
# One rule per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# These are sent to the model with every question. Edit them in the website's
# "Business rules" tab or in this file, then the next question uses them.

Revenue or sales means SUM(order_items.quantity * order_items.unit_price). Never use products.list_price for money that was actually charged; list_price is only the catalogue price.
Exclude orders with orders.status = 'cancelled' from revenue, sales, spend and order counts.
Net revenue means revenue minus SUM(returns.refund_amount) for the same order items.
Profit means SUM(order_items.quantity * (order_items.unit_price - products.unit_cost)) on non-cancelled orders.
The date of a sale is orders.ordered_at. "In 2025" means ordered_at >= '2025-01-01' and ordered_at < '2026-01-01'.
Online orders have orders.store_id = NULL, so use a LEFT JOIN to stores when a question covers all channels.
An active customer has customers.is_active = 1.
A customer's region comes from the store of their orders (orders.store_id -> stores.region_id -> regions.id); customers have no region column.
